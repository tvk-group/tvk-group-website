# EnteleKRON Financial-Layer Website Claims Policy

## Source content

Website implementations must consume the reviewed locale source in content/entelekron-financial-layer.<locale>.json. Text must be professionally translated from the approved source; automatic or placeholder translation is not acceptable for legal, financial, risk, reserve, redemption, licensing, or customer-asset copy.

## Allowed roadmap language

Use planned, architecture, research, testnet, or jurisdiction-gated when supported. State that TVKUSD is not issued and that ENK remains separate.

## Prohibited unsupported claims

Do not publish "bank", "bank account", "deposit", "licensed worldwide", "fully licensed", "guaranteed", "risk free", "insured", "fully reserved", "audited", "instant redemption", "global payments", or "available in every country" unless the exact entity, product, activity, jurisdiction, period, scope, and evidence are displayed.

## Activation evidence

Production copy is driven by the signed product/activity/entity/jurisdiction registry. Missing, expired, revoked, or conflicting evidence produces a non-active status.

## Türkiye

Crypto-asset payment routes remain disabled under the current Türkiye baseline. Website copy may describe sandbox architecture and separately lawful non-payment work, but cannot invite or imply crypto payment use in Türkiye.

## Release gate

Product, compliance, legal, security, localization, and website owners review the page together. The English and Turkish source files in this PR are canonical source drafts; existing localized pages must not be partially overwritten before translation parity and review.
